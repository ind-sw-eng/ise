      <p class="text-lg text-itu-ink mb-4">
        ISE pairs teams of software engineering students with real companies for a full semester. The company brings a genuine development challenge and a Product Owner; the team works it in two-week sprints and hands over working software at the end.
      </p>
      <p class="text-lg text-itu-ink">
        Partner companies pay no fee for that. The course is funded by the IT University of Copenhagen together with its sponsors, and keeping it free is what lets us say yes to a startup with a good problem and no budget as readily as to a large organisation. Sponsorship is what holds that door open.
      </p>

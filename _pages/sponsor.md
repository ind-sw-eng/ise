    <div class="max-w-3xl">
      <h2 class="font-display text-4xl font-bold mb-4 text-itu-purple">What you are sponsoring</h2>
      <p class="text-lg text-itu-ink mb-4">
        Industrial Software Engineering puts bachelor and master students together in teams &mdash; eight to ten developers with a master&rsquo;s student as Scrum Master &mdash; and gives each team a real project from a real company. Two weeks of kickoff, eleven weeks of sprints, around 16 teams running at once, with industrial agile coaches alongside them.
      </p>
      <p class="text-lg text-itu-ink">
        Companies bring the projects and a Product Owner; they pay no fee for the team&rsquo;s work. Sponsorship is what funds the course around those projects &mdash; the Scrum training and certification, the simulation, the events where everyone meets &mdash; and it is why we can take a good project from a company of any size.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

          </li>

      <div>
        <h2 class="font-display text-4xl font-bold mb-4 text-itu-purple">Worth knowing</h2>
        <dl class="space-y-4">
          <div class="border-l-4 border-itu-mint pl-4">
            <dt class="font-semibold text-itu-purple">Intellectual property</dt>
            <dd class="text-itu-ink">Each side owns what it creates; joint work is owned in proportion to the contribution. IP created by a student belongs to that student, and ITU has a template agreement if you want to take it over.</dd>
          </div>
          <div class="border-l-4 border-itu-mint pl-4">
            <dt class="font-semibold text-itu-purple">Confidentiality</dt>
            <dd class="text-itu-ink">ITU is bound by the confidentiality clause of the Danish Public Administration Act, and specific NDA terms can be added per activity. A quality award sponsor also signs an addendum covering what is disclosed during project presentations.</dd>
          </div>
          <div class="border-l-4 border-itu-mint pl-4">
            <dt class="font-semibold text-itu-purple">Students come first</dt>
            <dd class="text-itu-ink">Students must always be able to sit their exams; that cannot be signed away. A student can mark a project confidential and ask for a closed oral exam.</dd>
          </div>
          <div class="border-l-4 border-itu-mint pl-4">
            <dt class="font-semibold text-itu-purple">Who else is in the room</dt>
            <dd class="text-itu-ink">Competitors may be present, as sponsors or as participating companies. We may also limit sponsorship to companies relevant to the aims of the course, and other sponsors may have negotiated different arrangements.</dd>
          </div>
        </dl>
